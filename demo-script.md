# BriefMe — Demo Narration Script

Read each numbered section aloud as one take. The sections match the on-screen
segments of demo.mp4 one to one; demo-timings.md gives the exact window for each.
Numbers are spelled out where pronunciation matters.

## Section 1 — Landing (intro)

This is BriefMe. Paste the mess, get the brief. Teams run on documents nobody re-reads: long meeting transcripts, articles, email threads. The decisions and action items buried inside are what people actually need, and they get missed. BriefMe pulls them onto one page. It is powered by the Google Gemini A I, and right now it is running in its clearly labelled demo mode, an offline extractor with the same output shape, so the demo can never break.

## Section 2 — Load the meeting sample

BriefMe ships with three sample documents. I will load this one: a weekly product sync about a checkout revamp. It is a real meeting transcript, with numbers, tangents, and decisions scattered through the discussion. Everything you are about to see is extracted from exactly this text.

## Section 3 — Generate and walk through the brief

One click, and here is the brief. The T L D R at the top sums up the meeting in two sentences. Then the key points. Then the decisions, stated plainly: patch the payment reload bug first, and keep guest checkout as the default. And the section that saves real time: action items in a table, each with an owner and a due date. Daniyal investigates the Android payment bug by Wednesday. Ayesha sets up the abandonment dashboard by Friday. Open questions are listed too, and nothing is invented: if the document does not state it, the brief does not claim it.

## Section 4 — Copy as Markdown

The whole brief copies as Markdown in one click, ready to paste into a team chat, a doc, or a ticket.

## Section 5 — Validation and close

And if you paste something too short to brief, BriefMe tells you straight away instead of inventing a brief from nothing. One document in, decisions and action items out. That is BriefMe, built by Muhammad Khizar for Build With A I: Basics.
